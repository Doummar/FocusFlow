# FocusFlow

FocusFlow is a comprehensive study companion for Anki that combines a focus timer, interactive study history, fatigue tracking, goal tracking, and long-term learning analytics into a single, integrated dashboard.

FocusFlow keeps a long-term record of your study activity, allowing you to revisit your learning history even as your collection changes over time.

---

## Quick Start

1. Open the FocusFlow panel.
2. Start a study session using the built-in focus timer.
3. Explore your study history by clicking days, weeks, or months in the heatmap.
4. Open **Settings** to customize FocusFlow to your own workflow.

---

# Features

## Permanent Study History

FocusFlow keeps a permanent record of your study activity, making it easy to look back at your learning progress even after cards, notes, or entire decks have changed or been removed.

---

## Focus Timer

Stay focused with an integrated Pomodoro-style timer.

Features include:

- Custom study and break durations
- Automatic pause when reviewing stops
- Optional adaptive session lengths
- Session goals and end conditions
- Custom notification sounds

---

## Fatigue Detection

FocusFlow monitors your study behaviour throughout each session and can suggest a break before fatigue begins to affect your performance.

Fatigue can be detected using:

- Response speed
- Timing consistency
- Answer quality

Sensitivity and monitored signals are fully configurable.

---

## Interactive Heatmap

Visualize your learning with a GitHub-style contribution calendar.

Features include:

- Multiple colour themes
- Review Count, Effective Time, or Study Quality metrics
- Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly summaries
- Due-card forecasting
- Click any day to inspect detailed statistics
- Built-in notes and reminders
- Current and longest streak tracking

---

## Study Analytics

Gain insight into your study habits with statistics such as:

- Study Quality
- Effective Study Time
- Review Time
- New Cards
- Review Cards
- Streaks
- Session Summaries

---

## Goals

Track your progress using customizable study goals.

Depending on your settings, FocusFlow can display:

- Goal completion
- Remaining cards
- Estimated time remaining

---

## Customization

FocusFlow is designed to be highly customizable.

Customize nearly every aspect of the add-on, including:

- Heatmap appearance
- Colour schemes
- Timer behaviour
- Fatigue detection
- Toolbar layout
- Statistics display
- Notification sounds
- Multiple configuration profiles

---

# Why FocusFlow?

FocusFlow brings together several study tools into one place, making it easier to understand not only **how much** you study, but also **how** you study.

Instead of switching between multiple add-ons, FocusFlow combines focus sessions, study analytics, goals, reminders, fatigue tracking, and long-term study history in a single interface designed to feel like a natural extension of Anki.

If you're looking for a dedicated heatmap, **Review Heatmap** is an excellent community add-on. Likewise, if you only need a lightweight Pomodoro timer, **Minimalist Pomodoro** is another great option.

FocusFlow takes a different approach by bringing these ideas together with long-term analytics, fatigue tracking, goals, reminders, and permanent study history in one integrated workflow.

# Configuration

All settings are available from:

**Tools → Add-ons → FocusFlow → Config**

or from the built-in **Settings** dialog.

Settings are organized into the following categories:

- Timer
- Fatigue
- Heatmap
- Display
- Toolbar & Sound
- Measurement

Most settings include tooltips explaining what they do.
